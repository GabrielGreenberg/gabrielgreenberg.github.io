Indexicality

About the seminar

This seminar will investigate the nature of indexicality. We will start with the semantics of indexicality, with an emphasis on the sign, content, and truth. It involves the discussion of three kinds of semantics representation: icon, symbol, and index. We will explore different types of indexicality, including I, you, that, mental indexicality, pointing, arrows, perception, context-sensitivity. We will examine the nature of content: reference, sense, concepts, character, content and beyond.
